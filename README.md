# Proposal FOSDEM DataStreaming DevRoom 2027

Data powers modern applications, but moving it reliably, efficiently, and in real time remains a complex challenge. The Data Streaming DevRoom brings together the open-source communities, maintainers, contributors, and practitioners building the technologies that enable data in motion.

We welcome talks on **stream processing**, **Change Data Capture (CDC)**, **event-driven architectures**, **data integration**, **observability**, **orchestration**, **real-time analytics**, and **AI-driven data pipelines**. The focus is on practical experiences, innovative solutions, lessons learned, and the future of open-source data streaming.

This is a **vendor-neutral** forum designed to foster collaboration across projects, organizations, and communities. Whether you maintain an open-source project, operate streaming platforms at scale, or build applications powered by real-time data, join us to discuss the ideas and technologies shaping the next generation of data streaming.

## Process

The Data Streaming DevRoom is intended to be a **community-driven** initiative. To ensure broad representation across the data streaming ecosystem, the organizing team will actively reach out to maintainers, community leaders, and contributors from relevant open-source projects and organizations.

## Organizing Committee

The organizers may invite individuals from projects and companies active in the data streaming space to join the DevRoom Organizing Committee. Organizers are responsible for defining the DevRoom scope, promoting the Call for Papers (CFP), coordinating activities, and ensuring the event remains open, inclusive, and vendor-neutral.

Individuals interested in joining the Organizing Committee may also self-nominate.

To apply:

1. Fork this repository.
2. Update `index.md` by adding your:
   - Name
   - Organization (if applicable)
   - Project(s) you represent
3. Add your detailed information to `members.md`. 
4. Open a Pull Request with your proposed additions.

The existing organizers will review applications and may contact candidates for further discussion.

### Selection Principles

All organizers and CFP Selection Committee members are expected to:

- Act in the best interest of the DevRoom and its attendees.
- Maintain a vendor-neutral perspective.
- Evaluate talks based on technical quality, relevance, originality, and community value.
- Disclose potential conflicts of interest when reviewing submissions.
- Foster a welcoming and collaborative environment for all participants.

Our goal is to build an organizing and review team that reflects the diversity of the open-source data streaming ecosystem and ensures a fair, transparent, and high-quality selection process.
